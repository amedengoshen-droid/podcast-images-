Cloudinary settings

Only needed when you choose “Resize and upload”. These settings are saved on this device. Never enter your Cloudinary API secret here.

The preset name must match Cloudinary exactly, including capital letters, spaces and hyphens. Its signing mode must be Unsigned.

Manage your presets

Create a named size once, then select it whenever you need it. You can still choose download or upload each time.

Image resizing happens in your browser on your device. When you upload, the image is sent to Cloudinary using your unsigned preset. Your Cloudinary password and API secret are not used.
